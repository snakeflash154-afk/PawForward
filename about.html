About us

We rescue stray animals, nurse them back to health, and help them find homes.

Why we started

PawForward began with a few neighbors feeding the strays on their street. Today we rescue dogs and cats, treat their injuries, vaccinate them, and place them with families who are ready to love them.

Every animal that comes through our doors is checked by a vet, fed well, and given time to trust people again.

Meet the animals

What we do

Three jobs, one goal: a safe home for every stray.

Rescue

We respond to reports of strays and injured animals and bring them to safety.

Rehabilitate

Vet care, vaccines, spay and neuter, and daily love until they are healthy and ready.

Rehome

We match each animal with an adopter and follow up after they go home.
